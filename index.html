MedTech Demo

MedTech leverages State Space Models (SSMs) to model heart dynamics as continuous-time controlled ODEs, ensuring robust handling of irregular sampling and precise capture of physiological signals.

Optimized for edge inference, the model is ultra-lightweight (4 MB) with latency as low as 5 ms — running entirely in your browser.
